## Timeline

### 1. Foundations
- Created the Next.js 15 + TypeScript project and `config/event-rules.ts`. This file holds every rule: capital, assets, factors, costs, margin, mandate, scoring, difficulty, validator, pool and pitch settings. Nothing else hard-codes a rule.
- Created a new free Supabase project. I didn't reuse your existing project (`type-beat-toolkit`), so the two apps stay separate.

### 2. Simulation engine (`packages/engine`)
- **Price model:** a seeded, deterministic factor model with 7 factors (global equity, China/HK equity, rates, USD, credit, commodities, risk-off). It covers 21 instruments, and USD/HKD is held inside the 7.75–7.85 peg band.
- **News:** each headline's shocks are spread over a few ticks, depending on difficulty. Slow-burn events build over about 80 ticks.
- **Trading:** market and limit orders, shorting, leverage, margin calls, forced liquidation, financing costs, and optional transaction costs (off by default).
- **Mandate and scoring:** the client mandate is checked every tick. A breach costs score only after it lasts longer than a grace period. The composite score combines return, Sortino, drawdown and mandate, using the weights in config.
- **Replays:** replaying a session's input log reproduces it exactly, down to identical prices and fills.
- **Report:** reaction time per headline, hit rate, best and worst trades, breaches, turnover, and a breakdown by news type and asset class.
- **Validator:** checks schema, plausibility (clamps or rejects oversized shocks), shock direction against sentiment, event density per difficulty, and near-duplicates in the pool.
- **Offline scenario generator:** builds validated scenarios from templates, so the pool is never empty.

### 3. Content
- **Hand-written scenarios:** Soft Landing (Easy), Tariff Shock (Medium) and Stagflation Squeeze & Peg Jitters (Hard).
- **Clients:** 10 hand-written client briefs.
- **Historical replays:** 5 episodes with real daily prices fetched from Yahoo Finance: 2008 GFC, 2015 China crash, 2020 COVID, 2022 Fed hikes and 2023 SVB. Each has hand-written headlines plus anonymised versions for disguise mode.
- **Seeded database:** 15 synthetic scenarios, 5 historical episodes and 10 clients are loaded into Supabase.

### 4. Trading screen
- **Briefing:** a client card, the starting regime, the event rules, a factor cheat-sheet, and an opening-allocation editor with a live mandate check. A 3-minute countdown starts the session.
- **Terminal:** news feed (flashes on new headlines, colour-coded by source), quotes with sparklines, a price chart with news and fill markers, order ticket, positions/working orders/fills, risk panel (Sortino, drawdown, VaR, leverage, margin, mandate meters, exposure by asset class), and the clock and score.
- **Hotkeys:** B/S, 1–5 size presets, Enter, L (market/limit), F (flatten), X (cancel all), ↑/↓, and Space (pause, Easy only).
- **Quick links:** `/play/demo?autostart=1` jumps straight into a live Medium session.

### 5. Debrief, leaderboard, progress, settings
- **Debrief:** score breakdown; your equity curve against the optimal path; a drawdown chart; a headline-by-headline table; missed opportunities; best and worst trades; breaches.
- **Leaderboard:** filters by difficulty and mode, with sortable columns.
- **Progress:** score trend, weak spots by news type, P&L by asset class, and averages by difficulty.
- **Settings:** personal overrides for weights, capital, costs and briefing time, plus a password change.

### 6. AI worker (`worker/`, runs on your Mac)
- Runs `claude -p` on your Claude subscription. It strips `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` so nothing is billed to an API account.
- Listens to the `ai_jobs` queue through Realtime, with polling as a backup. It writes a heartbeat, retries failed jobs, re-queues stale ones, and runs coach and judge jobs before pool top-ups.
- **Job types:** scenario generation (with a blind second sentiment pass and up to 3 validated attempts), client generation (constraints must be satisfiable), the coach, and the pitch judge (with a whisper.cpp transcription fallback).
- Includes a `launchd` plist, `npm run worker:dry` and `worker/ai-smoke.ts`.

### 7. Pitch mode
- A 5-minute recorded pitch with a live transcript (Web Speech API). The audio is uploaded to a private bucket.
- The judge asks 3–5 follow-up questions, one at a time, and reads each one aloud. You get 2 minutes per answer.
- Ends with a scorecard on 5 criteria, each with a justification and one improvement.

### 8. Deployment
- Code pushed to GitHub, then a Vercel project created and linked with the Supabase environment variables.
- Vercel Authentication turned off on request, so the site is publicly reachable but requires sign-in.

### 9. Team mode (2 players)
- **Shared input log:** a new `session_inputs` table records every order, cancel and "end". The server's sequence number fixes a single global order.
- **Lockstep engine:** every browser applies the same inputs to its own engine (`packages/engine/src/team.ts`). If an input arrives late, the engine rebuilds and replays to the current tick in a few milliseconds.
- **Shared clock:** both players compute the clock from the server time and the session's start time.
- **Shared briefing:** the opening allocation is edited together live. Either player can start the clock, and a guard stops it from being started twice.
- **Live screen:** shows who's online and what they're looking at, alerts for your teammate's trades, and a "Who" column in the fills tab. **End & score** ends the session for both players.
- **Per-player results:** each player gets their own score row, debrief and coach.

### 10. Private accounts
- **Allowlist:** a database trigger blocks every sign-up that isn't on the list. Guest access was removed and every page requires sign-in.
- **Sign-in:** pick your name (Nicolas or AG) and type a password, with no email to type. Accounts use internal `@wealthathon.invalid` addresses that never receive mail.
- **Passwords:** each player can change theirs in Settings. Set to short passwords chosen by Nicolas, on request.

### 11. Individual scores in team sessions
- **Attribution by trade:** each fill belongs to whoever placed it. A player's P&L is what their own fills earned, marked to market every tick. The opening allocation (a joint decision) and forced liquidations go to a "Team" bucket.
- **Checks out:** player buckets + team bucket + financing add up exactly to the team P&L (unit-tested).
- **Same formula as the team:** each player gets a composite score (return, Sortino, drawdown, mandate) computed on their own contribution path. A mandate breach is charged to the player whose trade triggered it; breaches caused by market drift are charged to nobody.
- **Logged per player:** each player's score row stores their individual composite, the four components, P&L, trade count, hit rate and average reaction time. Their debrief stores an individual report.
- **Shown everywhere:**
  - Trading screen: a live per-player P&L in the top bar.
  - Debrief: an "Individual scores" table and contribution chart.
  - Leaderboard: a "My score" column and a team/solo filter.
  - Progress: the trend, weak spots and by-difficulty view all use your own trades.
  - Coach: critiques the requesting player on their own trades.

## Decisions and deviations from the original spec

- **Ticks per session:** a session is always 1,200 ticks, so the price path doesn't depend on play speed. That gives 0.75 s ticks at 15 minutes and 2.25 s at 45 minutes, slightly outside the requested 1–2 s.
- **Prices:** every instrument is quoted as HKD per unit, with no FX conversion. Currency only matters for the HKD-heavy and USD-heavy mandate rules.
- **Historical gaps:** the 2008 replay has no USD/HKD or Brent data, and AIA history wouldn't download, so those aren't tradable in those replays.
- **Players:** the spec planned for a 3-person team with roles. Team mode is built for **2 players** as requested. Roles are stored but not enforced, and both players can trade.
- **No pause in team mode**, so neither player can freeze the other's clock.
- **Guest mode removed** when the app became private.
- **Round 1 format:** research found HKU's page says Round 1 also includes uploading a **strategy video**. Pitch mode doesn't have a recorded-video mode yet.
